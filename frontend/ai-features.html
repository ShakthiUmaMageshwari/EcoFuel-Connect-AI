What Our AI Can Do

Every feature is live — explore the interactive tools below.

#01
Recommendation Engine
Suggests best fuel based on your city, budget, and use-case (home/vehicle/industrial).
#02
Location-Based Matching
Matches buyers with the nearest verified sellers using city-to-city distance scoring.
#03
Demand Prediction
Predicts which cities will need more fuel next month using trend + weather modifiers.
#04
Price Prediction
Suggests optimal pricing for sellers based on market data, season, and weather demand.
#05
Route Optimization
Calculates the shortest delivery path to minimize fuel consumption and delivery cost.
#06
Cost Comparison AI
Compares petrol/diesel/LPG vs EcoFuels — shows exact ₹ savings per month.
#07
Carbon Footprint AI
Calculates exact CO₂ saved by switching fuels. Shows monthly, annual, and tree equivalents.
#08
User Behavior Analysis
Tracks searches and purchases to deliver smarter personalized fuel recommendations.
#09
AI Chatbot Assistant
Answers questions about fuels, cost savings, installation, government schemes, and more.
#10
Smart Classification
Auto-detects fuel type and use-case from seller product descriptions using NLP.
#11
Fraud Detection
Flags suspicious pricing, fake sellers, and anomalous stock quantities automatically.
#12
Supply-Demand Matching
Balances supply (sellers) against demand (buyers) to show market gaps by area.
#13
Weather-Based Prediction
Adjusts demand forecasts based on season (summer/monsoon/winter) for each fuel type.
#14
Waste-to-Energy AI
Estimates biogas or biomass output from agricultural/organic waste types and quantities.
#15
Energy Consumption Insights
Shows platform-wide usage trends, CO₂ saved by fuel type, and monthly order patterns.
#16
Personalized Awareness AI
Educates users about sustainable fuels based on their search behavior and purchase history.
#17
Area-wise Intelligence
Shows which cities have highest eco-fuel adoption, supply gaps, and fuel mix distribution.
#18
Smart Notifications
Alerts buyers when prices drop, new sellers arrive nearby, or demand spikes in their city.
