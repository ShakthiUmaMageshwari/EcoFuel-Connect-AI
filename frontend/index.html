India's Energy Challenge

The Oil Crisis Risk

India imports 85% of its crude oil. Wars in oil-producing regions cause price spikes of 30–50%, directly affecting petrol, diesel & LPG prices for millions.

The Local Solution

India generates 700 million tonnes of agricultural waste annually — enough to supply 100% of cooking fuel demand. This waste is being thrown away!
